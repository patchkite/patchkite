# Code of Conduct

Patchkite follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). By participating in this project, you agree to abide by its terms.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on the work. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers privately through a [GitHub security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) or by direct message to a member of the [patchkite organization](https://github.com/orgs/patchkite/people). Reports are handled confidentially.
